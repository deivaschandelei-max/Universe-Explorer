    Add to your Home Screen

    1. Tap the Share button in the browser toolbar.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. Universe Explorer then opens full screen and works offline.

    Install before you save any progress. The Home Screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. To move progress across, export a backup in Safari and import it in the app.
